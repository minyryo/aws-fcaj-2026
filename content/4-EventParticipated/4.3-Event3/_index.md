### Event Summary

The FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week was an intensive hackathon run in collaboration with **JI Fund**. Within a very tight window (roughly 24–48 hours), teams had to brainstorm, build, and pitch a working AI-powered product to a panel of judges.

### Key Takeaways

- **Scope management is decisive.** Under severe time pressure, the winning approach was to keep the scope small and solve one specific pain point (an MVP) rather than endlessly adding features.
- **Execution beats theory.** A great idea counts for little without a working demo — a functional product to show the judges was the single biggest determinant of success.
- **Teamwork means setting the ego aside.** Building under sleep deprivation and pressure forced teams to divide roles clearly and trust each other's calls; the alternative was team conflict that could sink the project.

### Team Presentations & Highlights

**1. One Team — 🥇 First Prize**
An *AI-powered conversational ordering agent* for F&B brands (e.g. KFC). The team built a multi-channel chatbot that lets customers order food directly inside messaging apps like Zalo — no separate app, no account needed. Using **Amazon Bedrock** and **AgentCore**, the bot keeps user context, handles natural conversation, and processes orders securely, removing friction from the ordering flow.

**2. Signal Scout — 🥈 Second Prize**
A *competitive-intelligence and business-strategy agent*. It scrapes and analyses scattered public data (financial reports, structural changes) about competitors to help strategists judge whether adopting a similar model would be profitable — automatically forecasting revenue upside and risk, and cutting the time executives spend on manual research.

**3. Team Plan**
An *AI architecture generator for Solutions Architects*. Aimed at urgent client requests, it turns natural-language prompts plus business policies into an instant AWS architecture diagram on draw.io — and also estimates cost and generates deployable Infrastructure-as-Code (Terraform).

**4. Team 3K — "Sheper"**
An *AI-powered crowd tracking and management system*. Using **YOLO** and **ByteTrack** computer-vision models, it analyses real-time camera feeds in crowded venues (airports, supermarkets) to detect and predict congestion, then autonomously alerts staff and suggests strategies to clear bottlenecks.

**5. Six Pillars**
An *Adaptive Workflow Engine* for Anti-Money-Laundering (AML) in financial institutions. Acting as a co-pilot for analysts, it automates the tedious investigation of false-positive transaction alerts — using multiple sub-agents to cross-reference KYC profiles, money-flow patterns, and sanction blacklists — cutting 3-hour investigations to minutes while keeping a human in the loop for final approval.

### Outcomes and Value Gained

- **Reinforced the MVP discipline** I'm applying to our own project — under a deadline, scoping tightly (core booking + payment first, extras later) is exactly how we've kept the court-booking build on track.
- **First-hand exposure to the agentic-AI / Bedrock ecosystem** — seeing production-style agents built on Bedrock + AgentCore in 48 hours broadened my sense of what's now feasible, and how our SNS/Lambda notification path could later evolve toward conversational interfaces.
- **A concrete model of high-pressure teamwork** — the "set the ego aside, divide roles, trust decisions" lesson mirrors how our five-person team splits ownership (BE / FE / CI-CD) and is a habit worth carrying beyond the internship.
