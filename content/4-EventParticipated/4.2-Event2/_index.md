### Event Description

Swinburne Cloud Mastery 2026 focused on bridging the gap between cloud theory and how the industry actually builds and ships. The programme was organised around three themes:

- **Enterprise cloud in practice** — a look at how real organisations design, architect, and deploy their solutions on AWS, moving from whiteboard designs to running production systems.
- **Cloud career pathways** — industry professionals, together with a Swinburne Vietnam alumnus, shared how they built their careers in cloud computing: the skills that matter on the job, and which certifications actually move the needle for early-career engineers.
- **Industry culture** — a look at the iconic Bitexco Financial Tower offered a window into the innovation-driven culture of the cloud industry and the working environment of the companies operating in it.

---

### Outcomes and Value Gained

- **Grounded the classroom concepts in real deployments** — hearing how enterprises take a design to production reinforced the exact path our team is walking (architecture → CI/CD → deploy), and validated design choices like putting the workload behind a load balancer with autoscaling.
- **Clearer certification roadmap** — the career panel's advice on *which* certifications are worth prioritising directly shaped how I plan my AWS study alongside the internship project.
- **Perspective on industry expectations** — the culture segment gave a concrete sense of the standards (professionalism, clean architecture, teamwork) that cloud employers look for, which I can carry into how I document and present this project.
